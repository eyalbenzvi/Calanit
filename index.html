About Be’eri Printers

A trusted partner for complex card operations

Be’eri Printers is an established printing and card personalization business, with a long record of secure, high-quality delivery for banks, government bodies and major commercial organizations.

Working in environments that demand rigorous data security and production quality, Be’eri Printers combines industrial capacity with the precision that the financial and identity card sectors require.

From initial data intake to final delivery, every process is designed to protect cardholder data, maintain chain-of-custody integrity and meet international compliance requirements.

What we do

Personalization services

End-to-end card personalization — from secure data handling to physical production and distribution. Every service is delivered within a controlled, audited environment.

Graphical personalization

High-speed printing of card-unique data: cardholder name, PAN, expiration date and service code.

Cardholder data personalization

Accurate application of cardholder data with full data integrity and a complete audit trail.

Encoding & chip personalization

ISO/IEC 7811 magnetic stripe encoding, contact chip personalization and contactless configuration for payment and identity cards.

Embossing & indent printing

Embossing to ISO/IEC 7811 character sets, with indent printing and thermal printing of cardholder data.

Card-to-carrier matching

Automated verification confirms every card is matched to the correct carrier before dispatch, with mismatches rejected and reconciled.

PIN & secure information handling

Secure generation and delivery of PIN mailers and other sensitive credentials under strict access control.

Mailing & fulfillment

Mailing services covering address validation, envelope insertion and postal preparation.

Packaging & insertion

Custom, branded packaging and envelope insertion that protect card integrity in transit.

Sorting

Sorting by branch, region, postcode, card product or client-specified criteria, with per-consignment manifests.

Secure dispatch & chain of custody

Distribution-ready preparation: manifests, documentation and signed chain-of-custody records.

Welcome packs & regulatory inserts

Personalized welcome packs, regulatory inserts and ancillary documents within the card package.

Process

How it works

A structured, secure workflow from project brief to delivery, designed around operational transparency and data protection at every stage.

01

Customer requirements

Project scope, volumes, card specifications and security requirements are agreed and documented.

02

Secure data & card intake

Cardholder data is received over encrypted transfer and acknowledged; card stock is booked in, counted and stored under controlled access.

03

Data preparation

Data is validated, reconciled and prepared for production in a secured processing environment.

04

Personalization

Cards are encoded, printed and embossed with full batch traceability throughout production.

05

Quality control & matching

Every card is verified against its source record, and card-to-carrier matching is reconciled before release.

06

Packaging & delivery

Cards are packed in sealed, tamper-evident consignments and dispatched to client specifications, with full documentation.

Security & compliance

Security and quality built into every process

For banks, financial institutions and government bodies, card personalization is a security-critical operation. Be’eri Printers operates a production environment built around protecting cardholder data, card integrity and business continuity at every stage.

ISO 9001:2015
ISO/IEC 27001:2022
PCI CP&P

Card personalization is governed by the PCI Card Production and Provisioning Security Requirements — Physical and Logical — which are assessed per facility and are distinct from PCI DSS. Our current scope and status are provided on request.

Certificates, assessment scope statements and current validity dates are provided in full with our RFQ pack, and are available on request.

  • Encrypted data transfer and controlled intake
  • Access-controlled, monitored production area
  • Role-based access control and activity logging
  • Documented quality assurance procedures
  • Batch-level traceability and reconciliation
  • Confidentiality agreements and staff screening
  • Controlled handling of personalization data
  • Signed chain of custody on every consignment
