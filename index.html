What We Do

Personalization Services

End-to-end card personalization — from secure data handling to physical production and distribution. Every service is delivered within a controlled, audited environment.

Variable Data Printing

High-speed printing of unique data — names, account numbers, expiry dates — tailored to each individual card.

Cardholder Data Personalization

Accurate application of individual cardholder information with full data integrity and audit trail.

Encoding

Magnetic stripe, chip programming and contactless personalization for payment and identity cards.

Data Embossing & Printing

Physical embossing and thermal printing of cardholder data to industry specifications.

Card-to-Carrier Matching

Automated verification ensuring each card is matched to the correct carrier document before dispatch.

PIN / Secure Info Handling

Secure generation and delivery of PIN mailers and sensitive credentials, compliant with security standards.

Mailing & Fulfillment

Complete mailing services including address verification, envelope insertion and postal preparation.

Packaging

Custom and branded packaging solutions designed to protect card integrity during transit.

Sorting

Automated and manual sorting by branch, region, card type or any client-specified criteria.

Preparation for Distribution

Distribution-ready preparation — manifests, documentation and chain-of-custody records.

Additional Personalized Materials

Personalized inserts, welcome letters and ancillary documents as part of the complete card package.

Process

How It Works

A structured, secure workflow from project brief to delivery — designed around operational transparency and data protection at every stage.

01

Customer Requirements

Project scope, volumes, card specifications and security requirements are agreed and documented.

02

Secure Data & Card Intake

Cardholder data and card stock are received through controlled, secure intake procedures.

03

Data Processing

Data is validated, formatted and prepared for production in a secured processing environment.

04

Personalization

Cards are encoded, printed and embossed with full batch traceability throughout production.

05

Quality Control & Matching

Every card is verified against its data record. Card-to-carrier matching confirms accuracy.

06

Packaging & Delivery

Cards are packaged, sorted and dispatched per client specifications with full documentation.
